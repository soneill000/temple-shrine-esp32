flash the badge — no CLI needed

Plug your 2026 DEF CON badge into a USB port, then click the button below. Your browser (Chrome, Edge, or Opera on desktop) will prompt you to pick the serial port and take it from there.

If the flash button won't connect: hold the BOOT button on the badge, tap RESET, release BOOT, and try again. This forces the ROM bootloader.

Prefer the command line? Download the individual bin files from the latest release and use esptool.py.
